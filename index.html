Subscription

Football Manager Pro is a yearly subscription with a 3-day free trial. Manage or cancel it any time in your Apple ID settings (Settings › your name › Subscriptions), at least 24 hours before renewal. To restore a purchase, use Restore Purchases on the paywall.
