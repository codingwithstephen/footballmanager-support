Football Manager: Playbook – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Football Manager does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The team, roster, play, schedule and practice information you enter is stored only on your device (and in your own device backups, if you have them enabled). There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles purchase data.

Questions? Open an issue on our support page.
